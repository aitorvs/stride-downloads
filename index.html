A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your first run.

How do I install it?

On Mac, unzip the download and move Stride.app into Applications. On Windows, extract the entire Stride folder and open Stride.exe inside it; keep the included files together. A shortcut makes it easy to open again.

Which Mac download do I need?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it lists an Apple M-series chip, choose Apple Silicon. If it lists an Intel processor, choose Intel.

Where does my data go?

Your activities, notes, and saved Garmin session live on your computer. Stride connects to Garmin when you sync. It does not upload your running data to this website or send anything to an AI automatically. GPS is excluded from dashboard exports unless you choose to include it.

Can I use Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Copy a session report or attach its JSON in your existing conversation. For direct, read-only access, connect Claude Desktop or ChatGPT through AI connection setup. Claude Desktop needs no API key or tunnel; the optional ChatGPT tunnel uses an OpenAI runtime key.

How do I close or update Stride?

Closing the browser tab leaves the app running. Use Quit Stride in the dashboard to save pending notes and stop it. To update, quit the app and replace it with the new download. Your library stays in its separate data folder.
